The pipeline

From resume to submitted
in four steps

A deterministic pipeline that maps your profile to each form, generates grounded answers, and never submits without your explicit sign-off.

1
Upload your resume
Drop a PDF or DOCX. ApplyAgent reads every line and builds a structured profile with stable fact IDs — no hallucinated employers, no invented dates.
2
Paste the job URL
The agent opens the application form, detects every field, classifies each question, and maps your profile facts before writing a single character.
3
Review every answer
Open-ended questions are answered using only your verified profile. Sensitive fields and low-confidence answers are flagged and held for your approval.
4
Approve and submit
One click issues the approval token. The agent fills the form and submits. Nothing moves without your explicit go-ahead.
Built right

Not just another autofill

Every design decision in ApplyAgent exists to prevent the failure mode of AI job tools: confidently wrong answers and silent submissions.

Fact-grounded answers
Every generated answer traces to a verified fact in your profile. No employer you didn't work for. No skill you didn't list.

Human approval gate
The agent cannot submit without your signed token. Sensitive fields (visa status, disability, salary) are always held for review, never auto-filled.
Always in control
Deterministic field mapping
